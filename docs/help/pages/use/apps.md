# Apps

Fadenstack has apps of its own for other places you work: the desktop agent, the Office add-ins, the Nextcloud app and the Chrome extension. When your administrator offers one, the **Apps** page shows where to get it and how to connect it to your organisation's server.

## Open the page

Open the chat's menu and select **Apps**, or go to `/apps` on your organisation's server. The page lists only the apps your administrator has offered.

## What it shows

For each app:

- the download;
- the newest version;
- the end of its support;
- how to connect it to this server, with the server's address.

## Get an app and connect it

1. On **Apps**, select the app's download link.
2. Install it from the page that opens.
3. In the app, enter the server's address shown on **Apps** if it asks, and sign in with your own account.

Your IT department may already have set the server's address for you, for example for the Office add-ins and the Chrome extension.

The Nextcloud app is different: your Nextcloud's administrator installs it for everyone. Once it is there, open Fadenstack in Nextcloud and sign in.

## Where the downloads go

A download opens the site where the app is published: GitHub, the Chrome Web Store or the Nextcloud app store. That site sees your address (IP), as with any link. The link does not tell it which server you came from.
